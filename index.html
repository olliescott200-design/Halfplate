Find restaurants that fit a GLP-1 appetite.

People on Ozempic, Wegovy and Mounjaro still eat out, but they want half the food, more protein, and a price that matches the smaller plate. Halfplate shows which restaurants offer exactly that, rated by the people who eat there.

Source: National Restaurant Association, GLP-1 Usage and Restaurants, May 2026 (US survey).

The gap

Menus aren't built for small appetites. A diner who can only manage half a main still pays full price, takes home a box they won't finish, or guesses which venue will let them order the entrée as a meal. That knowledge lives in forum threads, not in any app.

  • Small Plate rating

    Community scores for portion options, nutrition, value and how staff handle a "smaller please" request.

  • Price that fits the plate

    Every small meal shows its price, what you'd save against the full serve, and a "Fair half-price" badge.

  • Protein-first ranking

    Results match your protein target, appetite size and budget per meal, not just distance.

  • Just what to ask for

    Diner-sourced tips like "kids' steak is fine for adults" or "entrée can be plated as a main".
